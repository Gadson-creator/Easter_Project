About Eastern Hope

Steel you can build on

Eastern Hope Ltd is one of the leading importers and distributors of steel products in Rwanda, importing from the region as well as outside the region in search of quality steel products to satisfy our customers' needs.

Eastern Hope imports mainly steel construction materials and wholesales them to retailers and construction companies at the most affordable price in the country. In a country like Rwanda developing at a high rate of speed, with the growing infrastructure needs of the nation, Eastern Hope offers high quality steel products for the safety of the nation as a whole. At Eastern Hope we treat our clients with respect and ensure to deliver the best customer care and quick service.
